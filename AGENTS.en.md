# Contributor instructions

[中文](AGENTS.md) | English

- Read README.en.md, CODEX_START.en.md and docs/roadmap.en.md first.
- Distinguish proposals, successful builds, runtime observations and hardware acceptance.
- Do not query boards, open serial ports, reset, flash or rewire at this stage. Hardware operations require later explicit authorization.
- Preserve existing firmware and network configuration; port numbers alone do not establish device identity.
- Record source, version and license before importing third-party code. Do not commit credentials, device MAC addresses, private configuration or flash backups.
- Work on the current stage and record experiments; label unmeasured results as such.
- Update Chinese and English documents together. Keep the project independent; do not add cross-project dependencies, links or collaboration relationships.
